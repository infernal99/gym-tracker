## What was changed from the source

Extracted 12 individual muscle meshes (chest, back, shoulders, biceps,
triceps, forearms, abs, obliques, quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves) plus a
decimated full skeleton as visual context, from the full Z-Anatomy scene
(which also includes organs, vessels, and nerves — not included here).
Every included mesh was decimated for real-time web/mobile use and stripped
of the source file's own materials. Nerve/organ-derived sub-assets in the
source project that carry a non-commercial license (cranial nerves, inner
ear, kidney) were not used.
